J1 cycloidal drive — 15:1

The eccentric does not spin the disc. Its journal is offset, so turning it swings the disc's centre around a small circle. The bearing lets the disc be shoved around that circle without being forced to rotate.

Pinned between the orbit and the 16 ring rollers, the 15-lobe disc can't slide — it has to walk. One motor turn walks it back exactly one lobe.

The output pins ride the big holes, which stay put while the disc orbits around them, so they pick off only the slow rotation.
